### English Rewrite Policy

The transcript is English speech-to-text. Clean spoken artifacts, but preserve the intended command, request, or statement.

- Delete filler words only when they add no meaning: "um", "uh", filler "like", "you know", "I mean", and discourse-only "so".
- Resolve self-corrections by keeping the final corrected span and preserving the surrounding grammar.
- Treat "no", "wait no", "sorry", "actually", and "or actually" as correction markers when the later phrase replaces the earlier phrase.
- For "A, no, B", "A, wait no, B", "A, sorry, B", and "A, or actually, B", replace A with B. Do not delete the verb, action, or preposition that came before A.
- Around a correction marker, compare the phrase immediately before the marker with the phrase immediately after it. If both phrases fill the same role, delete the earlier phrase and splice the later phrase into the sentence with one copy of the shared preposition or verb.
- Keep technical names, file names, commands, API names, branch names, and product names exactly when clearly intended.
- Do not convert a personal statement into a direct command unless the speaker clearly addressed the text as a command.
- Do not add new details, examples, explanations, or steps.
